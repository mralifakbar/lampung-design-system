Hero New

The top of a product or landing page: one headline, one line under it, at most two actions, and the product itself beside or below. The product is shown working, not as a picture of a screen.

Usage

Use it when

  • The first screen of a landing, product or pricing page that sits in front of an app.
  • A launch page for one feature, where the feature can run live next to the headline.

Use something else when

  • The top of a page inside an app: use Page header. Inside an app people already know where they are; they need the title and the actions.
  • The one next step for a signed-in person: use Spotlight.
  • A closing ask at the bottom of the page: use CTA band.

Where it goes. Directly under the top nav, full content width (1200 px, the same gutter as every marketing section). hero--split puts the copy and the product side by side from 1024 px; below that the product follows the copy. On phones the actions stack when they do not fit.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
section.heroRequired. The grid; aria-labelledby the title.
.hero--splitCopy left, product right, from 1024 px.
.hero--centerCopy centered over a wide product below.
.hero--enterThe copy settles once on first load, 60 ms apart, then nothing moves on its own.
.hero__copyRequired. Holds title, lede, actions and note.
h1.hero__titleRequired. 40 to 68 px, tracking −0.042em, balanced lines. One sentence that says what people get.
.hero__quietOptional second sentence inside the title in the subtle color.
p.hero__ledeOne to three sentences with the facts behind the headline.
.hero__actionsOne primary button, one secondary at most.
.hero__noteA short line under the actions: a shortcut, a free plan, a requirement.
.hero__mediaThe product: live components on a feature stage, a working demo.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
hero--splitCopy and product side by side from 1024 px.
hero--centerCentered copy, max 860 px wide.
hero--enterOne short stagger on first load.

States

StateHow
first loadWith hero--enter the copy and then the media rise 4 px out of a 4 px blur over --d-smooth, 60 ms apart.

Motion and microinteractions

  • hero--enter: lds-blur-in over 560 ms on --ease-enter, delays 0, 60, 120, 180 ms for the copy and 240 ms for the media. It plays once; the whole stagger stays under 400 ms.
  • Nothing loops. The live product moves only when someone uses it.
  • Reduced motion: a 120 ms fade with no delay.

Accessibility

  • The title is the page h1; the section is labelled by it.
  • Live components in the media keep their own labels and keyboard behavior; say in a caption what can be tried.
  • The title color is --text-strong, the quiet part --text-subtle (6.6:1 on white, 7.7:1 on the dark canvas).

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Say the outcome in the title and the facts in the lede.
  • Show the real product, running.
  • Name the primary button for what happens ("Start free", "Choose a plan").

cancelDon't

  • Park a pill badge above the title that repeats it.
  • Use a screenshot, a mock terminal or an illustration where the product could run.
  • Add a third button or a background effect.
