Question 4 asks for the net force, not the applied force. You added the 20 N push but left out friction, which acts against it with 6 N. The net force is 14 N, so the acceleration is 14 N divided by 7 kg, which is 2 m/s².
So friction always points against the motion?
09:14
Against the motion, or against the direction it would move if nothing held it. A book resting on a tilted table has friction pointing up the slope even though it is not moving yet.

Third normal form

A table is in third normal form when every column depends on the key, the whole key, and nothing but the key. In practice that means splitting out any column that really describes another column.

How to check a table

  1. Find the primary key, for example student_id.
  2. For each other column, ask whether it describes the key or something else.
  3. Move columns that describe something else into their own table.

Normalize until it hurts, then denormalize until it works.

A saying among database teachers

Most course systems stop at third normal form; going further rarely pays off for read-heavy data 1. See the next lesson for indexes.
