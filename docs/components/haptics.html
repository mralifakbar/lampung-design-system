Haptics

A short vibration on phones that support it, always together with a visible change, never alone. Four kinds: light for a tap that changed something, medium for a gesture that committed, success when something finished, error when something was refused.

Penggunaan

Pakai saat

  • Adding feel to a moment that already has visible feedback: a like, a switch landing, a swipe that commits, a long press that opens a menu.
  • Confirming an outcome the person waited for: an upload finished, a confirm completed.

Pakai yang lain saat

  • Telling the person something with no visual change: pakai Toast. Haptics are never the only signal.
  • Refusing input: pakai Shake. It already buzzes the error pattern.
  • Every button press: pakai Press feedback. The visual sink is enough.
  • Reducing motion: pakai Reduced motion.

Letaknya. Called from code at the moment of the event, after a user gesture. The system components already call it; you only call it for new moments.

Contoh

Varian dan ukuran

Kelas atau atributKapan
light8 ms. A tap that changed something: like on, confirm armed.
medium14 ms. A gesture that committed: swipe delete, long press opening a context menu.
success10, 40, 18 ms. Something finished: confirm done, upload complete, thinking done.
error12, 30, 12, 30, 12 ms. Refused: shake(), likeRevert(), a failed save.

Gerak dan mikrointeraksi

  • Timing: fire at the same moment as the visual change (pointer down for presses, the commit frame for gestures), never after an animation.
  • One haptic per event. Do not stack a light tap under a success pattern.
  • Reduced motion does not turn haptics off; data-lds-haptics="off" on <html> does (offer it as a setting).

Aksesibilitas

  • Never the only signal: every haptic pairs with a visible and, where it matters, an announced change.
  • Runs only after the person has interacted with the page (navigator.userActivation), so pages never buzz on load.
  • Respect a "Vibration" or "Haptics" setting in the product with data-lds-haptics="off".

Lakukan dan hindari

check_circleLakukan

  • Use the four kinds as named; do not invent patterns.
  • Let system components fire their own haptics.

cancelHindari

  • Buzz on every press or on scroll.
  • Use a haptic to tell the person something the screen does not show.
  • Fire haptics from timers or background events.

Perilaku dan kode

HTML biasa. haptic(kind) uses navigator.vibrate when available, after user activation, unless data-lds-haptics="off" is on <html>. Called by like, confirm, swipe commit, context menu long press, shake, thinking done and upload complete.

React. Dari lampung-design-system/react.

import { notify } from 'lampung-design-system/react';

notify({ title: 'Upload finished', icon: 'check_circle' }, 'success'); // toast plus a success haptic

Terkait

MikrointeraksiShakeA refusal: the control shakes twice, quickly and with shrinking swings (360 ms), and the reason appears in words next to it. The shake catches the eye at the exact place; the words say what to do.MikrointeraksiLikeEndorsing something is optimistic: the heart fills, pops and throws seven sparks the moment it is pressed, and the count rolls up, before the server answers. If the server refuses, it rolls back with a shake and says why.GerakPress feedbackThe control answers the moment a finger or mouse goes down: it sinks a little in 90 ms and springs back on release. Depth follows size, so small things sink more and wide things barely move. Every system control already does this; you only choose the right control.GerakReduced motionWhen people ask their device for less motion, the system keeps every state change and drops the travel: transitions become 120 ms linear changes, keyframe animations play once in 1 ms, dialogs and menus stop scaling, shimmer and sparks stop, flights and scroll reveals are skipped. Spinners keep turning because they report work.
