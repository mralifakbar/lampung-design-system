Dialog

A small centered layer that interrupts to ask for one short input or decision, then gets out of the way. Use rarely: most tasks belong on the page, in a sheet or in a drawer.

Usage

Use it when

  • One short input that must happen before going on: rename a course, set a due date, name a new folder.
  • A short decision with two clear outcomes that is not destructive.
  • Inside a contained region that is already positioned (.viewport), such as a canvas, a preview or a phone frame.

Use something else when

  • Confirming something destructive or irreversible (delete, remove, discard): use Alert dialog.
  • A page-level form or report over the whole window, or a dialog that must sit above everything: use Modal. Native <dialog> in the top layer; turns into a bottom sheet on phones.
  • A task on a phone that keeps the page in view (choose, filter, attendance): use Sheet.
  • Filters or details beside a list on wide screens: use Drawer.
  • Telling people something happened: use Toast.
  • A few extra fields or options anchored to a button: use Popover.

Where it goes. Centered in its positioned container (.viewport, or a position: fixed; inset: 0 wrapper for the page), 340 px wide, above a scrim. On phones prefer a sheet; for the whole page prefer a modal.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.scrim[data-lds-scrim="id"]Required. The dim layer behind; a click on it closes the dialog.
.dialog[role=dialog][aria-modal=true]Required. The layer, with an id, data-lds-layer and aria-labelledby its title.
.dialog__closeOptional close icon button in the top right, with data-lds-close.
.dialog__titleRequired. The question or task, as an <h2> or <h3>.
.dialog__bodyOne or two sentences of context.
.dialog__actionsRequired. Two buttons sharing the width: secondary (dismiss) then primary.
[data-lds-open="id"]Required. The trigger, with aria-haspopup="dialog".

States

StateHow
.is-openVisible and interactive; the scrim with the same id gets .is-open too. Set by openLayer.
closedaria-hidden="true" and inert, invisible after the exit finishes.

Motion and microinteractions

  • Enter: opacity over --d-fast (160 ms) on --ease-enter; transform from translateY(4px) scale(0.98) to none on --spring-smooth (560 ms). Add .is-entering first for a deeper start (translateY(8px) scale(0.96)).
  • Exit: shorter and quieter, opacity and transform over --d-exit (180 ms) on --ease-exit, then visibility: hidden. Both are transitions, so closing mid-entrance reverses from where it is.
  • Scrim: fades in over --d-standard (240 ms), out over --d-exit.
  • Reduced motion: no transform at all, opacity only in 120 ms.

Accessibility

  • role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby the title (and aria-describedby the body when it carries the point).
  • On open, focus moves to [data-lds-autofocus] (or [autofocus]), else to the first focusable element. Tab and Shift+Tab are trapped inside while aria-modal="true".
  • Escape closes the top layer only; the scrim and every [data-lds-close] close it too. Focus returns to the element that opened it.
  • The trigger carries aria-haspopup="dialog".

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Ask one thing; title it as the question or the task.
  • Name the primary button for its outcome ("Save name", "Publish").
  • Autofocus the field, or the primary for a non-destructive choice.

cancelDon't

  • Open a dialog from a dialog; finish or close the first one.
  • Put long forms, tables or scrolling content in it; use a modal, sheet or a page.
  • Use it to confirm a delete; that is an alert dialog.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. [data-lds-open="id"] calls openLayer(id, trigger); [data-lds-close] inside and [data-lds-scrim="id"] call closeLayer. Escape closes the top layer. Events lds:open and lds:close fire on the layer. Programmatic: openLayer(id), closeLayer(id or element).
