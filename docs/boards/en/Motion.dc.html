Foundations · Motion

Motion

Calm by default. Things arrive on a spring with no overshoot, leave on a short accelerating curve, and can be redirected mid-flight because every transition starts from where the element is now.

Respond on press

Feedback starts on pointer down, in 90 ms. Waiting for release feels dead.

Enter slow, leave fast

Entrances take 240 to 560 ms. Exits take 160 to 180 ms and never bounce.

Same path back

A sheet that rose from the bottom drops back to the bottom. Menus grow from their trigger.

Bounce is earned

Overshoot only follows a gesture that carried momentum, like a flicked sheet.

Curves and springs

Press Play, then press it again before the pucks land. Springs reverse from their current position; that is the interruptibility you feel in every component.

Reduced motion

With the system setting on, springs and slides become 120 ms cross-fades, overlays stop scaling, and loops play once. Spinners keep turning because they report work in progress.
