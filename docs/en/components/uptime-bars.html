Uptime bars

One thin bar per day (or hour) in a row, colored by how that period went: no issues, degraded, or down. The pattern of incidents over 90 days reads at a glance; pointing at a bar, or using the arrow keys, says what happened that day.

descriptionView as Markdown

Usage

Use it when

  • The history of a running service on a status or infrastructure page: 90 days of a website, an API, sign-in, payments.
  • Any daily pass, partial, fail record where streaks and clusters matter: nightly backups, scheduled imports, daily check-ins.

Use something else when

  • The state of a service right now: use Status dot.
  • A quantity per day over months (hours, submissions): use Heatmap.
  • Events of one record in order: use Timeline.
  • A value over time: use Area chart.

Where it goes. One row per service in a card or a status page section, full width. The wrapper .uptime-box is a container: under 480 px it shows the last 30 days and the axis says so.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.uptime-boxRequired. Container for the row, its axis and the readout.
.uptime[data-lds-uptime]Required. The bars: role="img", an aria-label with the overall figure, tabindex="0" for the arrow keys.
iRequired. One bar per period, oldest first. .is-ok, .is-warn, .is-down, or no class for no data. data-label says the day and what happened.
.uptime__axisRecommended. Start and end of the period; .uptime__long and .uptime__short swap with the width.
.uptime__nowRecommended. The readout line: the current state at rest, the pointed day on hover or arrows.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
i.is-ok / .is-warn / .is-downNo issues, degraded, down: success, warning and danger colors. Leave the class off for days without data.
hours instead of daysSame markup with 24 or 48 bars; the axis says "24 hours ago" and "Now".

States

StateHow
pointed dayThat bar grows 12 % taller, the others fade to 45 %, the readout shows its data-label.
no data yetBars without a class in the track color, and the readout says when tracking started.
loadingOne .skel strip 32 px tall.

Motion and microinteractions

  • The pointed bar scales up on --spring-snappy; fading the others takes --d-fast. Moving along the row slides the emphasis, nothing jumps.
  • Reduced motion: emphasis changes in 120 ms, no scaling.

Accessibility

  • The row is one role="img" with the overall figure; with focus, Left and Right move day by day, Home and End jump, and each day is announced through the shared live region.
  • Every bar has words in data-label; color never stands alone, and the service status above repeats the current state with a status dot.
  • Bars are at least 2 px wide with 2 px gaps; the narrow view drops old days rather than squeezing them.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Show the overall figure ("99.91% uptime") next to the service name.
  • Say what happened, how long, in each data-label.

cancelDon't

  • Make each bar its own button or link; use one focus stop per row.
  • Show only green: if nothing went wrong, say "No incidents in 90 days" in the readout.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. data-lds-uptime on .uptime: pointer and arrow keys write the bar's data-label into the .uptime__now of the same .uptime-box and mark the bar .is-on; leaving restores the resting text.
