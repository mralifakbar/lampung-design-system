Link

Text that takes you somewhere: an <a href> with .link, medium weight in the accent text color, the underline fading in on hover. .link--external adds an arrow for other sites.

Usage

Use it when

  • Going to another page, section or site from inside a sentence or a short line of text: "Track its status", "Read the exam rules".
  • A quiet secondary path next to a form or card that does not change anything ("Forgot your password?").
  • Pointing to another website or a document that opens in a new tab (link--external).

Use something else when

  • The control does something on this page (save, delete, open a dialog): use Button. Use btn--plain if it should look light; it is still a button.
  • Long-form text with many links (articles, help pages): use Prose. Links inside .prose are styled automatically, underlined at rest.
  • A large destination tile with a title and description: use Link card.
  • A numbered reference to a source: use Citation.
  • Navigation between top-level sections of an app: use Sidebar.

Where it goes. Inline in body text, in helper lines under fields, at the end of card descriptions and alerts. On phones the link must stay at least 24 px tall with space around it; for standalone links on touch screens prefer a list row or a btn--plain.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
a.linkRequired. An <a href>. Accent text color, weight 500, underline offset 3 px and 1.5 px thick, transparent until hover.
.link--externalOptional. Appends a north_east arrow from the icon font after the text.
target="_blank" rel="noreferrer"With link--external when it opens a new tab; add hidden text that says so.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
linkInternal destination.
link--externalAnother site or a file that opens in a new tab.

States

StateHow
hoverUnderline fades from transparent to currentColor.
focusSystem focus ring around the text (3 px radius).
visitedNot styled; the system does not distinguish visited links.

Motion and microinteractions

  • The underline color transitions over --d-fast on --ease-standard. Nothing moves or scales.
  • Reduced motion leaves this as is (it is a color change).

Accessibility

  • Always an <a href>; never a <span> or <button> styled as a link.
  • Link text names the destination on its own ("Track its status"), never "click here" or "more".
  • For new tabs, add <span class="sr-only"> (opens in a new tab)</span> inside the link.
  • Color alone is not enough in running text with low contrast between link and body: the accent text holds 6.7:1 on the canvas and differs in weight; inside long articles use prose, which keeps the underline visible at rest.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Write link text that makes sense out of context.
  • Mark external links with link--external.
  • Keep links inside the sentence they belong to.

cancelDon't

  • Use a link to submit, delete or open a dialog.
  • Make a whole paragraph a link.
  • Use link--external for pages on your own site.
