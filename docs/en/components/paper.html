Paper New

A printable page shown as a sheet inside the app: an invoice, a receipt, a letter, a certificate. The text reflows with the width, so the preview stays readable on a phone, and it can be the only thing that prints.

Usage

Use it when

  • A live preview of a document people are editing in a form beside it: an invoice, a quote, an offer letter.
  • A record people print or save as PDF: a receipt, a payslip, a certificate of completion.

Use something else when

  • The details of a record inside the app, not a document: use Key-value.
  • Long rich text from an editor or a CMS: use Prose.
  • A self-contained subject among others in a grid: use Card.

Where it goes. In its own column beside the form on wide screens (sticky under the app bar), under the form on phones. Up to 816 px wide; it keeps a letter page proportion as its minimum height.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
article.paperRequired. The sheet: base color, a hairline ring, padding that grows with the width. aria-label names the document.
.paper__headBrand (.paper__brand with .paper__mark) at the start, .paper__doc (.paper__title and a badge) at the end.
dl.paper__metaNumber and dates; each dt.paper__label over its dd.
.paper__parties > .paper__partyFrom and to, each a .paper__label and lines of address.
table.paper__tableLine items; .num right-aligns figures.
.paper__totals > .paper__sumLabel and amount rows; .paper__sum--total for the amount due.
.paper__footPayment details and notes, pushed to the bottom of the page.
.paper--printModifier. When printing, only this paper prints, edge to edge, in the light theme.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
.paperA sheet on screen.
.paper--printThe one sheet that prints; everything else on the page is left out of the print.
.paper--receiptA till receipt: 400 px at most, as tall as its lines, 24 px padding, centered foot. Prints 80 mm wide.

States

StateHow
editingThe form writes into the paper on input; changed totals roll with rolling number.
empty fieldShow a muted placeholder in the paper ("Client name") rather than leaving a gap.
printingLight theme, no ring, no padding of its own; the page margins come from @page.

Motion and microinteractions

  • None of its own. Values that change while someone types may roll (rolling number); new line items enter with the list stagger once.

Accessibility

  • Use an article named by aria-label (the document and its number).
  • Real headings and a real table with th scope="col", so the preview reads in order.
  • In a live preview, do not announce every keystroke; the form fields are the source.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Keep it in the light hierarchy of the system: one weight step for totals, labels muted.
  • Give the amount due its own row with the currency.

cancelDon't

  • Scale the whole page down to fit a phone; let it reflow.
  • Put app controls inside the paper; they belong in the form.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. No hook needed. When a .paper--print is on the page, init() switches to the light theme for the print and back after.
