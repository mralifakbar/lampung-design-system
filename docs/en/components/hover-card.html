Hover card

A preview card under a link after a 600 ms hover or on focus: who a person is, what a course covers. It saves a click; it never holds the only copy of anything.

Usage

Use it when

  • A mention or link to a person, course or document where a quick look saves opening it: @lecturer, a course code in a table.
  • Dense lists where people scan many links and want context before choosing one.

Use something else when

  • A one-line name or shortcut for a control: use Tooltip.
  • Content people need to click into or edit: use Popover. A popover opens on click and stays until dismissed.
  • Commands on the item: use Dropdown menu.
  • Touch-first screens: use Sheet. There is no hover on touch; open a sheet or the page on tap.

Where it goes. 10 px below the link, aligned to its left edge, 280 px wide. Leave room below it; it does not flip.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.tip-hostRequired. Wraps the link and the card; shows the card on hover and focus.
a.linkRequired. The trigger: a real link to the full page.
.hovercardRequired. The card: 280 px, 16 px padding, elevated. Content is short: a title, one line, maybe an avatar.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
.hovercardOpens under the trigger, aligned to its start edge. Width 280 px, never wider than the screen minus 32 px.
.hovercard--endAligned to the trigger's end edge. Use it when the trigger sits in the right half of its column, so the card opens toward the free space instead of past the edge.

States

StateHow
:hover / :focus-withinShown after 600 ms; stays while the pointer is over the link or the card.
leavingHides after 150 ms, so the pointer can travel from the link to the card.

Motion and microinteractions

  • In: waits 600 ms, then opacity over --d-fast on --ease-enter and from translateY(-4px) scale(0.97) on --spring-smooth, growing from the top left.
  • Out: after a 150 ms grace, 120 ms on --ease-exit.
  • Reduced motion: the delays stay, transitions drop to 120 ms linear.

Accessibility

  • The link works on its own and goes to the full page; the card is a preview.
  • It shows on keyboard focus too. Link it with aria-describedby so the summary is read after the link name.
  • Keep interactive elements out of it: it is role="tooltip" and keyboard users cannot move into it.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Show two or three facts that help decide whether to open the link.
  • Use it where people hover links in bulk.

cancelDon't

  • Put buttons ("Follow", "Message") in a hover card.
  • Use it for anything people must read.
