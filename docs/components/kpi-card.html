KPI card New

One metric on a dashboard: what it is, its value, how it changed against a stated baseline, and optionally its recent shape as a sparkline or mini bars. The value is the point; the change always says what it is compared with.

Usage

Use it when

  • The first row of a dashboard: three to six metrics people check every visit (sales, sign-ups, refunds, reply time).
  • A metric whose recent shape matters as much as its value: add a sparkline (continuous) or mini bars (per day or week).
  • Several metrics of one subject joined into a strip (.kpi-grid) at the top of an analytics page.

Use something else when

  • Figures that summarize a page, with no change to report: use Stat row. A row of plain figures is quieter and needs no cards.
  • A metric with a question and a longer trend the reader studies: use Sparkline stat.
  • The reader needs values at specific dates: use Area chart.
  • Progress toward a goal: use Target bar.

Where it goes. At the top of a dashboard in an auto-grid (--min: 220px), or as one .kpi-grid strip. On phones cards stack full width; a strip goes to two columns under 760 px of its container and one under 400 px. Never more than six in a row.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.kpiRequired. article (or a.kpi--link when the whole card opens a report). Base background, --line ring, 16 px padding, 16 px radius.
.kpi__headRequired. .kpi__label (what is measured), optional .kpi__icon before it and one menu .icon-btn after it.
.kpi__valueRequired. 28 px tabular figure; a unit in small.
.kpi__metaRecommended. A trend delta and the baseline in words ("from $42,890 in October").
svg.kpi__sparkOptional. A full-bleed sparkline along the bottom edge: .series-area and .series-line in a 240 × 52 viewBox, preserveAspectRatio="none". role="img" with the trend in words.
.kpi__barsOptional. Mini bars, one i per period with --h as a percent; .is-on marks the current one.
.kpi-grid-wrap > .kpi-gridJoins cards into one ringed block with hairlines between them; --n sets the columns (2, 3, 4 or 6 so rows fill).

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
.kpi__sparkContinuous measure (sales, visitors): a filled line, no axes, no dots.
.kpi__barsCounted per period (sign-ups per day): mini bars with the current one in the series color.
.kpi__iconA quiet glyph tile that helps scanning a long row; not a colored tile.
.kpi--linkThe card is one link to the metric's report.
.kpi-gridJoined strip. --n: 2 | 3 | 4 | 6.

States

StateHow
loadingA .skel line for the label, a 28 px block for the value, one for the change; the card keeps its height.
no baselineLeave out the delta and say why in the meta ("First month").
errorThe value stays empty and the meta says "Could not load" with a plain Try again button.
value changed liveSwap the figure with rolling number; the delta rises in once.

Motion and microinteractions

  • First view (data-lds-grow on the row or the card): the sparkline wipes in from the left over 1 s on --ease-inout; mini bars rise from the baseline on --spring-smooth, 25 ms apart.
  • Link cards sink to 98.5 % in 90 ms on press and return on --spring-snappy.
  • Reduced motion: everything is drawn at once.

Accessibility

  • Each card is an article labelled by its .kpi__label (aria-labelledby), so a screen reader lists the metrics by name.
  • The delta carries the direction in words (.sr-only "up" / "down"); color only repeats it.
  • Sparklines and mini bars are role="img" with the trend in words; they are never the only place a value lives.
  • The menu button names its metric: "More actions for Course sales".

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Say what the change is compared with, in the meta.
  • Color the change by whether it is good, not by its direction.
  • Keep the same anatomy across one row: all with a sparkline or none.

cancelDon't

  • Put a chart with axes in a KPI card; that is a chart card.
  • Fill KPI cards with a color or put white text on a hue; the card stays on the base surface.
  • Show a percentage change on a base under 20; give the absolute change.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. Optional: data-lds-grow on the card or its grid plays the first-view entrance. Everything else is CSS.
