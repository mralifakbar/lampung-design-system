Tabs

Underlined tabs that switch between sibling sections of one page, each with its own panel. One ink line glides to the selected tab.

descriptionView as Markdown

Usage

Use it when

  • One record or page has sibling sections people visit one at a time: Overview, Applications, Documents, Discussion.
  • The sections hold different kinds of content, so each needs its own panel.
  • Four or more sections, or long labels: start-aligned tabs (.tabs--start) sized to their labels.

Use something else when

  • Switching between two to five views or filters of the same content (Week, Month; All, Unread): use Segmented control. Tabs swap sections; a segmented control changes a value.
  • Moving between pages of the app: use App shell.
  • A fixed sequence people must go through in order: use Steps.
  • Content people may want to read several sections of at once: use Accordion.
  • Destinations at the bottom of a phone screen: use Phone bars.

Where it goes. Directly under the page header of the thing they divide, full width of the content column, with the panel right below. On phones keep them under the title; if the labels do not fit, use .tabs--start inside a horizontal scroll rather than shrinking the text.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.tabs[role=tablist]Required. The row, with aria-label; a hairline runs along its bottom. Tabs share the width equally.
.tabs__inkThe 2 px line under the selected tab. First child; one per row.
.tabs__btn[role=tab]Required. A tab: aria-selected, aria-controls the panel id.
.tabs__countOptional count pill after the label, only when the number helps decide where to go.
.tab-panel[role=tabpanel]Required. One per tab, with the matching id; inactive ones are hidden. Rises in with a short blur.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
(default)Equal widths; two to four tabs with short labels.
.tabs--startTabs sized to their labels, aligned left, 4 px apart; four or more tabs or long labels.

States

StateHow
aria-selected="true"Strong text at weight 600; the ink sits under it; tabindex="0", the others -1.
hoverUnselected text darkens to strong.
focusSystem focus ring on the tab; arrow keys move selection and focus together.
.tab-panel[hidden]Inactive panels are removed from layout and from the accessibility tree.

Motion and microinteractions

  • Ink: one element translates to the selected tab on --spring-smooth over --d-smooth (560 ms); labels never animate on their own. A new click mid-glide retargets it from where it is.
  • Panel: the new panel enters with lds-blur-in over --d-standard (240 ms) on --ease-enter; the old one is hidden at once, so nothing cross-fades.
  • Label color eases over --d-standard. Reduced motion: transitions drop to 120 ms linear and the panel appears without the blur.

Accessibility

  • role="tablist" with aria-label, role="tab" buttons with aria-selected and aria-controls, role="tabpanel" panels with aria-labelledby and tabindex="0" when they hold no focusable content.
  • Roving tabindex: only the selected tab is in the Tab order. Left/Right (and Up/Down) move selection and focus, wrapping at the ends. The React binding also supports Home and End.
  • Counts are part of the tab name ("Applications 3"); keep them meaningful.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Name tabs with nouns for what is inside: "Applications", not "View applications".
  • Keep the selected tab when people come back to the page (put it in the URL or state).
  • Use .tabs--start once the labels stop fitting equally.

cancelDon't

  • Use tabs to filter a list or switch a chart; that is a segmented control.
  • Nest tabs inside tabs.
  • Put actions (Save, Delete) in the tab row.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. data-lds-tabs on .tabs selects a tab on click or arrow key, sets aria-selected and roving tabindex, moves and sizes .tabs__ink (equal widths), shows the panel named by aria-controls and hides the rest, and fires lds:change with { index, item }.

React. From lampung-design-system/react.

import { Tabs } from 'lampung-design-system/react';

const [tab, setTab] = useState<'overview' | 'applications'>('overview');

<Tabs label="Service sections" value={tab} onChange={setTab} align="fill"
  items={[{ value: 'overview', label: 'Overview' }, { value: 'applications', label: 'Applications', count: 3 }]} />
{tab === 'overview' ? <div className="tab-panel" role="tabpanel">…</div> : <div className="tab-panel" role="tabpanel">…</div>}
