Line chart

A line per series over time, on an SVG with receding gridlines and labels at the line ends. For trends across many points; at most four series, the second one dashed.

Penggunaan

Pakai saat

  • Showing how a value changes over time and comparing up to four series: your score against the class average, enrolments per month this year and last.
  • Ten or more points where the shape of the trend matters more than any single value.

Pakai yang lain saat

  • A tiny trend under one headline number: pakai Sparkline stat.
  • A few separate buckets to compare: pakai Bar chart.
  • Daily activity over weeks or months: pakai Heatmap.
  • The share of a whole: pakai Donut.
  • A timeline of events, not values: pakai Timeline.

Letaknya. Full width of a .chart-card in a report or the main column; the svg scales with the card. On phones show fewer periods (last 6 weeks) rather than squeezing labels, and keep the end labels.

Contoh

Anatomi

BagianFungsinya
svg[viewBox][role=img]Required. A fixed viewBox (1000 by 260 here) scaled to the card width; aria-label with the takeaway.
.axis-line / .axis-textGridlines (--hairline, 1 px) and tick labels (11 px --text-muted). Horizontal gridlines only.
.series-lineRequired. Series 1, 2.25 px, round joins, --series-1.
.series-line--2The comparison series: 1.75 px, dashed 4 4, --series-4. Dashes keep it distinct without color.
.series-areaOptional. An 8 % fill under series 1 only.
.drawOptional. Draws the line in once; pair with pathLength="1200".
.ctipOptional. The crosshair tooltip, absolutely positioned in a position: relative wrapper; you move it.

Status

StatusCaranya
hoverCrosshair line in --line-strong, dots on each series, .ctip with the week and values. Wire it yourself; see the example.
loading, empty, errorSee chart states.

Gerak dan mikrointeraksi

  • First view: .draw animates the stroke dash offset from 1200 to 0 over 1.2 s on --ease-inout. Without pathLength="1200" a long path draws only partly or finishes early.
  • The tooltip slides between weeks (left over --d-standard on --ease-standard) and fades over --d-instant.
  • Data updates replace the path without redrawing. Reduced motion: the line appears drawn and the tip jumps.

Aksesibilitas

  • The svg has role="img" and an aria-label that states the trend and the start and end values.
  • The crosshair is pointer only; offer the values in a .sr-only table or a visible "Show as table" toggle.
  • Series differ by more than color: the second is dashed, and each line is labelled at its end in text tokens.
  • The tooltip is aria-hidden; it repeats information that must be available elsewhere.

Lakukan dan hindari

check_circleLakukan

  • Label line ends directly ("You 84").
  • Keep the y range honest and say the unit in the subtitle.
  • Use the dashed style for the average or the previous period.

cancelHindari

  • Plot more than four series; fold the rest into Other or split the chart.
  • Smooth lines with curves that invent values between points.
  • Draw vertical gridlines or a boxed frame.

Perilaku dan kode

HTML biasa. Drawn from data: data-lds-chart with kind: "line" (or drawChart(el, config)) gives the draw-in, crosshair, keyboard reading, legend toggles and hidden table; see area chart. Hand-written svg: data-lds-crosshair on a .plot moves .cross and .ctip to the nearest [data-x] point.
