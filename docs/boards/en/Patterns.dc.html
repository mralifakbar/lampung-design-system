Flooding report

Kota Contoh has 14 spots prone to flooding. When heavy rain lasts more than two hours, water from blocked drains spills onto the roads, especially around the market and the bus terminal.

Follow-up

Clear the drains around the market and terminal before the rainy season, then install water level sensors at two spots.

Streaming text

What is a flood early warning system, in two sentences?
A flood early warning system watches water levels and rainfall from sensors in the city's rivers and drains. When the numbers pass the safe limit, residents in the affected area get an alert through the app and by SMS.

Prose

Online queues at the puskesmas

Residents get an online queue number when they register in the app, then arrive close to their turn. That keeps the waiting room from filling up and shortens the wait.

How to check your turn

  1. Find your queue number, for example A-027.
  2. See the estimated call time on the queue page.
  3. Arrive at least 15 minutes before your turn.

Arrive on time and your turn is kept.

Notice in the puskesmas waiting room

Puskesmas in Kota Contoh use online queues for the general clinic 1. Read the next guide for the dental clinic.
