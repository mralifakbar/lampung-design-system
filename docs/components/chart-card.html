Chart card Baru

The frame every dashboard chart sits in: the question as its title, what is measured under it, then optional actions (a range control, a menu), the headline figure with its change, the chart, and a footer with the source time or a link to the full report.

Penggunaan

Pakai saat

  • Any chart on a dashboard or report page: the card gives it a question, a period and a place for the one number people came for.
  • The reader should switch the period (7 days, 30 days, 3 months) or export the data without leaving the dashboard.
  • Several charts sit in one grid and need the same header, spacing and footer so the page reads as one system.

Pakai yang lain saat

  • One metric with its change and a tiny trend, in a row of four: pakai KPI card.
  • A few figures without any chart: pakai Stat row.
  • A chart inside an article or a report body: pakai Line chart. Put the plot straight in the text column with a caption; a card inside prose looks like an ad.
  • The chart is the whole page (a full report): pakai Page header. The page header carries the title and the range control; the chart fills the column.

Letaknya. In an auto-grid with --min: 320px on dashboards, or full width for a time series. Never inside another card. The header is a container: under 440 px the range control drops under the title and spans the card, the menu stays at the end of the title row.

Contoh

Anatomi

BagianFungsinya
section.chart-cardRequired. 20 px padding, 18 px radius, --line ring, 14 px between parts. aria-labelledby points at the question.
.chart-card__headOptional. A wrapping row: titles at the start, actions at the end. A size container for the range control.
.chart-card__titlesHolds .chart-card__q (the question, required) and .chart-card__sub (what is measured, over which period).
.chart-card__actionsOptional. A .seg.chart-card__range for the period and an .icon-btn menu. At most one button besides the menu.
.chart-card__figureOptional. .chart-card__big plus a trend delta and what it compares with.
.chart / .bars / .donut-chart …Required. The chart itself.
.chart-card__footOptional. A hairline, then the data time on the start and one plain link to the full view on the end.

Varian dan ukuran

Kelas atau atributKapan
.chart-card__rangeA segmented control for two to four periods. Add data-lds-chart-for="chartId" and data-set on each button to switch a data chart to config.sets[key]. More than four periods: a date range picker instead.
.chart-card__actions .icon-btnOne menu for export, copy link, full screen. Never a row of icon buttons.
.chart-card__figureThe headline number and its change. Leave it out when the chart has no single answer.
.chart-card__footData time and one link. Leave it out when there is nothing to say.

Status

StatusCaranya
loading, empty, errorThe card keeps its size and title; see chart states.
refreshingKeep the chart; a small spinner by the title while new data loads, then the marks move to it.
period changedThe chart animates; update the figure with rolling number and the delta in place.

Gerak dan mikrointeraksi

  • The period thumb slides on --spring-smooth (segmented control).
  • The chart moves from what is on screen to the new data over --d-considered (480 ms) on --ease-inout; a switch mid-way starts from the current shape.
  • The menu opens from its corner on the overlay recipe; the card itself never moves.
  • Reduced motion: the data jumps, the thumb cross-fades.

Aksesibilitas

  • Make the card a section labelled by its question, so the chart is a landmark people can jump to.
  • The range control is a group of toggle buttons with aria-pressed and a group label ("Period").
  • The menu button names its chart: "More actions for Orders chart".
  • The headline figure and delta are real text; the delta says the direction in words for screen readers when the arrow is the only cue (add .sr-only "up" or "down").

Lakukan dan hindari

check_circleLakukan

  • Write the title as the question the chart answers; put the period and unit in the subtitle.
  • Keep the same header order on every card: titles, range, menu.
  • Say once on the page that the numbers are examples.

cancelHindari

  • Nest a chart card in another card, or put two charts in one card.
  • Add a row of icon buttons; fold extra actions into the menu.
  • Use a select for two or three periods; the segmented control shows them all.

Perilaku dan kode

HTML biasa. data-lds-chart-for="chartId" on a .seg (buttons with data-set) or a native <select> (options with value) calls drawChart on that chart with config.sets[key]; see area chart for the chart config. A set may also carry figure, delta and note: they replace the text of [data-chart-figure], [data-chart-delta] and [data-chart-note] in the same card, which rise in once. The chart, legend, crosshair and table come from data-lds-chart.
