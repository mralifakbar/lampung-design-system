Lanes

Who is busy when: one row per person, room or resource, time running across, and blocks placed by their start and length. The question it answers is "who is free, and when", so gaps matter as much as blocks.

descriptionView as Markdown

Usage

Use it when

  • A roster where people book or plan around each other's time: doctors' hours today, field assistants, officer shifts.
  • Team time off across one to four weeks, with requests that are not approved yet.
  • Rooms, beds or vehicles booked by the hour or the day.

Use something else when

  • One person's or one team's events by month, week or agenda: use Event calendar.
  • Work moving through stages: use Kanban.
  • The history of one record: use Timeline.
  • Activity per day over months, as intensity: use Heatmap.

Where it goes. Full width of the content column, or at least eight of twelve columns: time needs room. On phones the box scrolls sideways with the name column pinned; the name column narrows and drops the avatar under 560 px of box width. Put a legend under it when blocks use more than one tone.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.lanes-boxRequired. The scroll box: white, ring, large radius. Give it tabindex="0", role="region" and an aria-label that names the span, so keyboard users can scroll it.
.lanesRequired. The grid. --cols the number of time columns, --col-min the narrowest a column may get before the box scrolls (56 px), --who the name column (200 px), --row-h the row height (56 px).
.lanes__head > .lanes__corner + .lanes__ticks > .lanes__tickRequired. What the rows are, then one tick per column: an hour, or a weekday with the date in b. .is-today in accent, .is-off on the quiet fill for weekends and closed hours.
.lanes__row > .lanes__whoRequired. The name, pinned on the left: an optional .avatar and .lanes__id with .lanes__name and .lanes__sub. A button.lanes__who opens the person.
.lanes__trackRequired. The time row with a hairline per column. Holds .lanes__off and .lanes__item blocks.
.lanes__itemA block at --from (columns from the start, fractions allowed) for --span columns, with a title span and an optional small line. A button when it opens something.
.lanes__offTime that does not count (weekend, closed): the quiet fill behind the blocks, placed like an item.
.lanes--now + --nowA line for the current time through every track, at --now columns, drawn behind the blocks.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
.lanes__itemSeries 1 tone. --2, --3, --4 for the other series colors, one per kind of block, with a legend.
.lanes__item--mutedBreaks and blocked time: neutral, quieter text.
.lanes__item--pendingNot settled yet (a request, a hold): no fill, a dashed edge in the tone.
.lanes__item--freeOpen time people can book: a dashed neutral edge and success text; tints on hover.
.lanes--nowShows the now line at --now.

States

StateHow
hover (button item)The tint deepens to 24 %.
pressedThe block sinks to 0.97 and springs back on --spring-snappy.
focusA 2 px focus ring outside the block, raised above its neighbours.
.is-newA block just added (an approved request, a new booking) grows in from its start once.
filteredhidden on a .lanes__row removes the whole row.
loadingKeep the head; skeleton rows of --row-h with a .skel name and two or three .skel blocks.
emptyAn empty state under the head: "Nobody is off in these two weeks".

Motion and microinteractions

  • Button blocks sink to 0.97 on press and return on --spring-snappy (360 ms); the tint changes over --d-fast.
  • A new block (.is-new) grows from its start edge on --spring-snappy, once, so the eye finds what changed.
  • Nothing else moves: the grid is a reference people read, not a show. Reduced motion: no press scale, no grow.

Accessibility

  • The box is a focusable region with a label that says what and when ("Doctors today, 07:00 to 17:00"), so it scrolls by keyboard.
  • Every block says its time in words where the position alone carries it: put the hours or dates in the small line, or in the button's aria-label ("Free from 13:00 to 14:30, book").
  • Tones repeat a word in the block or the legend; never rely on color alone. Pending says "Requested" or "Hold" in its title.
  • Interactive blocks are real buttons with a visible focus ring; read-only blocks are plain elements.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Start the columns where the day or period starts for the people reading it (07:00, Monday).
  • Show free time when the reader is looking for it, and make it bookable.
  • Mark weekends and closed hours with .lanes__off.

cancelDon't

  • Put more than four tones in one grid.
  • Squeeze it under about 480 px without letting it scroll; blocks shorter than an hour column lose their words.
  • Use it for one person's month; that is an event calendar.
