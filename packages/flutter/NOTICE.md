Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono were taken from the variable fonts in
[google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts) (`ofl/plusjakartasans`,
`ofl/jetbrainsmono`) and instanced to static weights (400, 500, 600, 700 and
400, 500) with fontTools, so Flutter selects weights reliably. Neither font
declares a Reserved Font Name; the family names are unchanged.

The icon fonts are generated by `scripts/build-icons.py` in the repository:
Solar `-outline` glyphs for `LDS Icons` and `-bold` glyphs for
`LDS Icons Fill`, reached by ligature names from `icons/icons.json`.
