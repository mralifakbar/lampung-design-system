Space, radius, depth

A 4 pt grid, radii that nest concentrically, and elevation that appears only when something actually floats above the page.

Spacing

Every gap is a multiple of 4. Related things sit 8 to 12 apart, groups 20 to 28, sections 48 and up.

  • Gutter20 on phone, 32 on tablet, 80 on desktop docs.
  • RowsList rows at least 48 tall, controls 44.
