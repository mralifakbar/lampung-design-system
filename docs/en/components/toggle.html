Toggle

A button that stays pressed to show a mode is on (bold, pin, mute). Alone it is a .toggle; several sit in a .toggle-group, either independent (bold, italic, underline) or one at a time (align left, center, right).

Usage

Use it when

  • A formatting or view mode that is on or off and applies to what you are working on: bold, italic, show grid, pin a note.
  • A compact toolbar of independent modes (a toggle group, several can be pressed).
  • An icon-led choice of one among two to four in an editor toolbar (alignment), where a segmented control would be too wide.

Use something else when

  • A setting that persists and applies everywhere (notifications, dark mode): use Switch. Switches live in settings lists with a label; toggles live in toolbars.
  • Choosing which view of content to show (list or grid, week or month): use Segmented control. It has a sliding thumb and text labels; use it for views.
  • Picking filters from a set of words: use Chips.
  • A one-off action that does not stay on: use Icon button.
  • Liking or bookmarking with a celebratory response: use Like.

Where it goes. In toolbars above the thing it changes: an editor, a canvas, a media player, a list header. On phones keep toggle groups to four buttons or fewer; they do not wrap.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.toggleRequired. A <button type="button"> with aria-pressed. 36 px tall alone, 30 px inside a group. Icon, text or both.
aria-pressed="true|false"Required. Drives the look: pressed is --accent-tint with --accent-text, and the icon switches to the fill style.
.toggle-groupWraps two or more toggles in one outlined box, 36 px tall. role="group" (independent) or role="radiogroup" (one at a time) with an aria-label.
aria-labelRequired on icon-only toggles ("Bold").

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
.toggleAlone: 36 px tall, 10 px radius.
.toggle-group .toggleIn a group: 30 px tall inside 3 px of padding (36 px total, the height of a medium button), 8 px radius.

States

StateHow
aria-pressed="true"--accent-tint fill, --accent-text color, fill-style icon.
hover--bg-tint and --text-strong when not pressed.
:activeSinks to scale(0.94) in 90 ms, back on --spring-snappy.
disableddisabled; the browser default dimming applies, so explain why nearby.

Motion and microinteractions

  • Press: scale(0.94) with a 90 ms duration, release on --spring-snappy over --d-snappy.
  • Pressed fill and color change over --d-fast on --ease-standard; the icon swaps to the fill font at once. There is no sliding indicator (that is the segmented control).
  • Under reduced motion the scale is a 120 ms change.

Accessibility

  • A <button type="button"> with aria-pressed. Its name stays the same when pressed ("Bold"), never "Bold on".
  • Independent group: role="group" + aria-label; each toggle is reached with Tab.
  • One-at-a-time group: role="radiogroup" + aria-label, buttons role="radio" with aria-checked. Arrow keys should move the choice; this is not built in, so wire it yourself or use a segmented control, which has it.
  • Icon-only toggles need an aria-label and should get a tooltip, ideally with the shortcut in a .kbd.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Use toggles for modes in toolbars; use switches for settings.
  • Keep the label the same in both states; the pressed look carries the state.
  • Offer keyboard shortcuts for formatting toggles and show them in the tooltip.

cancelDon't

  • Change the label to "On" or "Off".
  • Use a toggle group as page tabs or as a view switcher.
  • Mix toggles and plain action buttons inside one toggle-group.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. None in lds.js: set aria-pressed (and aria-checked for radios) from your click handler or framework state.
