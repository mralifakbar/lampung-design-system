Target bar

A horizontal bar for progress toward a goal: the fill is where you are, a marker shows where you should be (the goal, or today's pace), and an optional tint ahead of the fill shows the forecast. Words above say the value against the goal.

descriptionView as Markdown

Usage

Use it when

  • A figure measured against a target: local tax income against the quarter target, participants against the planned quota, vaccine doses against the weekly plan.
  • Comparing actual and expected pace: the marker sits where you should be by today, so falling behind is visible before the deadline.
  • A short list of goals side by side (one per puskesmas, per team, per month).

Use something else when

  • A task running toward done (upload, import): use Progress bar.
  • A quantity against a hard limit (storage, seats): use Meter.
  • A score in a square space: use Progress ring.
  • Comparing many categories with each other: use Ranked bars.
  • Parts of one whole: use Percentage bar.

Where it goes. In a chart card or a KPI card under the figure; several in a .target-list, 20 px apart. Full width of its container; the words wrap above the bar on phones.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.targetRequired. Sets --v (fill, percent of the track), --t (marker) and optional --f (forecast).
.target__metaRequired. .target__label and .target__val ("Rp 84.3 bn of Rp 100 bn").
.target__trackRequired. 12 px track, role="img" with the values in words.
.target__fillRequired. The actual value, in the accent; .target--success once the goal is met, .target--warning when behind pace.
.target__forecastOptional. The projected end value as a tint behind the fill.
.target__markRequired. A 2 px marker crossing the track, with .target__mark-label above it. .is-end aligns the label to the right when the marker is near the end.
.target__footOptional. Days left, pace in words.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
--v, --t, --fFill, marker and forecast positions as percentages of the track.
.target--successGoal met or ahead of pace.
.target--warningBehind pace; say by how much in the foot.
.target__mark.is-endMarker label aligned to its right edge (goal at 90 % or more).

States

StateHow
over the goalCap --v at 100 %, switch to .target--success and say the overshoot in words ("Rp 4.2 bn over").
no forecast yetLeave out .target__forecast and say why in the foot ("Forecast after week 2").
loadingA .skel line for the words and a 12 px .skel strip for the track.

Motion and microinteractions

  • Fill, forecast and marker move on --d-considered (480 ms) on --ease-inout when values change, from where they are.
  • First view (data-lds-grow): fills grow from zero on the same transition; the marker stays put.
  • Reduced motion: values switch in 120 ms.

Accessibility

  • The track is role="img" with value, goal and forecast in words; the meta line repeats the value as text.
  • Ahead or behind is said in the foot, never only by the fill color.
  • The marker label is real text, 12 px, with tabular figures.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Say the goal in the meta ("of Rp 100 bn") and what the marker means ("Goal", "Today").
  • Use the pace marker for goals with a deadline.

cancelDon't

  • Use a target bar for something without a goal; that is a ranked bar or a meter.
  • Stack more than one fill and one forecast in a track.
  • Color a fill red for being behind; warning is enough, words carry the rest.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. Optional: data-lds-grow on the bar or its list plays the first-view entrance.
