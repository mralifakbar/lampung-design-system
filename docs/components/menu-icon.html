Menu icon

Three bars that fold into a close icon when their button opens a menu, and unfold when it closes: the top and bottom bars meet and turn 45 degrees, the middle one shrinks away. The button stays in place, so the same spot opens and closes.

Penggunaan

Pakai saat

  • The button that opens and closes site navigation on small screens: the top nav toggle, the app shell drawer toggle.
  • Any button that opens a panel of navigation and also closes it from the same spot.

Pakai yang lain saat

  • A menu of actions on an item: pakai Dropdown menu. Use more_horiz or more_vert; it does not turn into a close icon.
  • Closing a dialog or sheet: pakai Icon button. A plain close icon.
  • Two icons for two states (play, pause): pakai Icon swap.
  • Opening a section in the page: pakai Height expand.

Letaknya. The leading edge of a top bar on phones (app shell), or the trailing edge of a site header (top nav). Always inside an icon button that carries aria-expanded.

Contoh

Anatomi

BagianFungsinya
.burgerRequired. 18 × 14 px, three <i> bars of 2 px. Must be a direct child of the element with aria-expanded.
[aria-expanded="true"] > .burgerThe folded state: bars 1 and 3 meet at the middle and rotate ±45 degrees; bar 2 fades and shrinks.

Gerak dan mikrointeraksi

  • Bars 1 and 3: top moves to 6 px and transform rotates ±45 degrees on --spring-snappy (--d-snappy, 360 ms), at the same time, so they cross in one motion.
  • Bar 2: fades over --d-fast and shrinks to scaleX(0.2) on --spring-snappy.
  • It is driven only by aria-expanded, so closing by Escape, a scrim tap or a followed link folds it back too, as long as the code updates the attribute (the app shell and top nav do).
  • Interruption: transitions, so a fast double press reverses mid-fold.
  • Reduced motion: the bars change in 120 ms with no spring.

Aksesibilitas

  • The button has aria-expanded, aria-controls and a stable aria-label ("Menu"); do not change the label to "Close" since the expanded state already says it.
  • Escape closes the menu and returns focus to this button.
  • The bars are aria-hidden.

Lakukan dan hindari

check_circleLakukan

  • Put .burger directly inside the button that has aria-expanded.
  • Update aria-expanded on every way the menu closes.

cancelHindari

  • Use it for item actions or for closing dialogs.
  • Swap two icons instead of folding the bars.
  • Move the button when the menu opens.

Perilaku dan kode

HTML biasa. CSS only, driven by aria-expanded. data-lds-shell-toggle (app shell) and data-lds-topnav-toggle (top nav) keep aria-expanded in sync for you.

Terkait

Below 760 px the links fold into a panel under the bar.NavigasiApp shellThe frame of an app: a sticky sidebar beside the content on wide screens, a top bar with a drawer below 960 px. One per app, at the root; one highlight glides to the current page.AksiIcon buttonA button that shows only an icon: 44 px target, 22 px glyph, round. It always has an `aria-label` and a tooltip with the same words.MikrointeraksiIcon swapOne glyph turns into another in place: the old one shrinks to half and blurs away while the new one settles on a snappy spring. For icons that say a new state (copied, playing, saved), not for decoration.
