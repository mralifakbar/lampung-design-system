Hints

A short tip that teaches a shortcut or a gesture, shown on the first few visits only and gone for good once closed. It gets quieter with use: by the time people know the trick, the hint has retired itself.

Penggunaan

Pakai saat

  • A gesture or shortcut that is not visible: swipe a row, press / to search, long press for more.
  • A feature that people miss in testing, where a one-line tip is enough.

Pakai yang lain saat

  • A guided tour pointing at parts of the screen: pakai Coachmark.
  • Something wrong or important now: pakai Alert.
  • Explaining a term next to a label: pakai Tooltip. Use an info tip.
  • A message after an action: pakai Toast.
  • Help that should always be there: pakai Prose.

Letaknya. Directly above or beside what it teaches: over the list that can be swiped, next to the search field with a shortcut. One hint per screen at most. Wrap it in a .collapse so closing it closes its space.

Contoh

Anatomi

BagianFungsinya
.hintRequired. data-lds-hint="<key>", data-max="3", role="note". Accent tint, icon, one sentence with the key words in bold.
.hint__closeRequired. data-lds-hint-close, aria-label="Hide this hint". Retires the hint at once.
.collapseRecommended wrapper: closing collapses the space instead of making the page jump.

Status

StatusCaranya
shownVisits counted so far are below data-max; the count goes up by one per page load (data-seen).
retiredAfter data-max visits, or as soon as it is closed: hidden, and the .collapse around it closes.
resetresetHints([keys]) clears the counts, so the hint shows again on the next load.

Gerak dan mikrointeraksi

  • The hint itself does not animate in: it is part of the page when the page appears.
  • Closing collapses the wrapping .collapse: rows to 0fr on --spring-smooth (560 ms) while it fades over --d-standard, so the content below glides up.
  • Reduced motion: the close takes 120 ms.

Aksesibilitas

  • role="note": read in place, not announced as an alert.
  • The close button has an aria-label. After closing, move focus to what the hint was about if focus was on the close button.
  • Say the gesture and its keyboard or button equivalent when there is one.

Lakukan dan hindari

check_circleLakukan

  • Give each hint a stable key and a small data-max (3 to 5).
  • Let people bring hints back from a help menu with resetHints().
  • Keep it to one sentence with the action in bold.

cancelHindari

  • Show more than one hint on a screen.
  • Use a hint for errors or announcements.
  • Hide the only way to an action behind a hint.

Perilaku dan kode

HTML biasa. [data-lds-hint] on init() counts the visit in localStorage (lds-hint:<key>) and hides the hint once the count reaches data-max; [data-lds-hint-close] retires it at once and closes its .collapse. resetHints(keys?) clears all or the named counts. If storage is blocked the hint always shows.
