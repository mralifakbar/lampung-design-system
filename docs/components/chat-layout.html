Chat layout Baru

The conversation column: messages stacked 20 px apart in .chat, the history scrolling above a composer that never scrolls away, and a Latest button that appears only when the reader has scrolled up. Newest at the bottom, always.

Penggunaan

Pakai saat

  • A conversation with an assistant or a tutor: the person asks on the right, answers fill the reading column, newest at the bottom.
  • A support or feedback thread where order and time matter and the person replies from the same screen.
  • A history long enough to scroll, where people read back and need a way to return to the latest message.

Pakai yang lain saat

  • A history of events with no reply box (grade changes, status updates): pakai Timeline.
  • Comments that each belong to a place in a document or submission: pakai List. Put them next to what they discuss, not in one column.
  • One generated answer placed inside a page with no back and forth: pakai Prose. Show thinking while it works and streaming text as it arrives.
  • A feed of notifications: pakai List.
  • A quick explanation of one question, in place: pakai Popover.

Letaknya. The main column of a page or a full-height panel beside the work it discusses (a resizable split on wide screens). The history is the scroller; the composer sits under it, outside the scroller, full width of the column. On phones the column is the whole screen and the composer sits above the keyboard with the safe-area inset (safe-bottom). Keep the column at most 640 px wide on wide screens so answers stay readable.

Contoh

Anatomi

BagianFungsinya
.chatRequired. The column of messages (flex, 20 px gap). Give it role="log" and a label.
.msgRequired. One message; see chat message for --me, --ai and the parts inside.
.msg--systemA centered line in footnote type for what changed in the conversation: a day break, someone joining, a mode switching on.
.chat__jumpA small secondary button, last child of .chat, with data-lds-jump="<scroller id>". Sticks 88 px above the scroller bottom and shows only while the reader is more than 160 px from the end.
.composerThe reply box under the scroller; see composer.

Status

StatusCaranya
emptyNo messages yet: an empty state with two or three starter chips above the composer.
scrolled up.chat__jump.is-visible once the scroller is more than 160 px from its end; it hides again at the bottom.
assistant workingThe last message is a thinking row, then the reply streams in (streaming text).
new messageA .msg.is-new rises 8 px and fades in once on insertion; history without is-new appears at once.

Gerak dan mikrointeraksi

  • Every .msg.is-new enters once with lds-msg-in (give is-new only to messages that arrive while the chat is open): from 8 px below and transparent, over --d-smooth (560 ms) on --spring-smooth. Nothing in the column moves when a message arrives except the new message itself.
  • The Latest button fades over --d-fast and rises 8 px on --spring-smooth; it is a transition, so scrolling back and forth reverses it from where it is.
  • Jumping scrolls with behavior: smooth; under reduced motion it jumps at once.
  • Messages rendered in bulk (opening an old conversation) also play their entrance at the same moment. Render history before inserting it, or accept one shared fade; never stagger a whole history.
  • Reduced motion: the entrance becomes a 1 ms fade (effectively instant) and the jump button cross-fades in 120 ms.

Aksesibilitas

  • Give .chat role="log" and an aria-label; new messages are then announced politely. While a reply streams, set aria-busy="true" on that message and remove it when done so the reply is read once, whole.
  • System lines are plain text (<p>), not live regions of their own; the log announces them.
  • The composer textarea needs a label (aria-label or a visible label); see composer.
  • The Latest button is a real button with a visible label. While hidden it is transparent but still focusable: render it only when needed if keyboard users report it.

Lakukan dan hindari

check_circleLakukan

  • Keep the composer outside the scroller so it never scrolls away.
  • Stop auto-scrolling when the reader has scrolled up; show Latest instead.
  • Use a system line for anything the conversation did on its own (mode, file, day).

cancelHindari

  • Animate the whole history in, or stagger old messages.
  • Put a timestamp on every message; group by day with system lines and show times on hover or in message meta.
  • Build a chat out of cards or list rows.

Perilaku dan kode

HTML biasa. Behavior comes from init(): [data-lds-jump="<scroller id>"] on the Latest button shows it while the scroller is more than 160 px from its end and scrolls to the end on click; [data-lds-composer] wires the composer (Enter sends, lds:send carries { text }). In React, render the same markup and call init(ref.current) in an effect after messages render.
