Meter

A segmented gauge for a level within a known range: storage used, seats taken, password strength. It shows how full, not how far along.

Usage

Use it when

  • A quantity against a limit: storage used, seats taken in a class, questions flagged out of an allowance.
  • A strength or quality level in a few steps, such as password strength (four segments).
  • A level that turns into a warning near the limit (.is-warn) and a problem at it (.is-full).

Use something else when

  • A task that advances to completion: use Progress bar.
  • A single score in a compact square: use Progress ring.
  • Shares of a whole across categories: use Percentage bar.
  • The person rates something: use Rating.
  • A grid of activity over days: use Heatmap.

Where it goes. Under a label that names the quantity, with the value in words (.progress-meta works as the header). In settings rows, under a password field, in a plan or storage card. Full width of its container; 160 to 320 px reads best.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.meter-segRequired. A grid of ten equal segments with 3 px gaps. Override grid-template-columns inline for another count (four for password strength).
iRequired. One per segment, 8 px tall.
i.onA filled segment (accent).
.is-warn / .is-fullOn .meter-seg: filled segments turn warning, then danger.
.progress-metaRecommended header: the label and the value in words.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
meter-segTen segments, accent fill.
meter-seg.is-warnNear the limit: filled segments turn to the warning color.
meter-seg.is-fullAt or over the limit: danger color.
grid-template-columns (inline)Another segment count, such as four for strength.

States

StateHow
levelAdd .on to the first n segments (round to the nearest segment).
warning.is-warn from about 80 % or a weak strength.
full.is-full at the limit, with the word "Full".

Motion and microinteractions

  • Each segment fades its color over --d-standard (240 ms) on --ease-standard. No sweeping and no bounce: the meter is a reading, not an event.
  • Several segments changing at once change together; do not stagger them.
  • Reduced motion: the color change becomes 120 ms linear.

Accessibility

  • Give the meter role="meter" with aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax, aria-valuenow, aria-valuetext and a name (aria-labelledby the label).
  • When the words beside it already say everything (password strength), mark the segments aria-hidden="true" and connect the word to the input with aria-describedby.
  • Warning and full must also be said in words; color alone fails for many people.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Write the value next to it ("6.2 of 10 GB").
  • Say what to do when it warns.
  • Keep ten segments for capacity and four or five for strength.

cancelDon't

  • Use a meter for a task in progress.
  • Fill segments with a gradient or different colors per segment.
  • Show more than twelve segments; switch to a progress bar look or a number.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. None. Toggle .on on the segments and .is-warn / .is-full on the meter from your code.
