Radio

Native radio buttons for exactly one choice out of two to five options that should all be visible. The dot springs in; arrow keys move between options.

Usage

Use it when

  • One answer from a short list where seeing every option helps: delivery method, exam session, answer options in a question.
  • Options are mutually exclusive and need a word or a line of explanation each.

Use something else when

  • Each option is a richer choice with a description, price or badge (plans, packages): use Radio card.
  • More than about five options, or space is tight: use Select.
  • A long list people type to find: use Combobox.
  • Switching a view or setting that applies immediately (Day, Week, Month): use Segmented control.
  • Several options can be on at once: use Checkbox.
  • A single on and off setting: use Switch.

Where it goes. In a form, stacked in a column 12 px apart inside a <fieldset> with a <legend> that asks the question. Two short options may sit side by side on wide screens; stack them on phones.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
fieldset + legendRequired. The question ("Where should we send your results?").
label.checkRequired. One option; the whole row is the hit area.
input[type=radio][name]Required. The native radio, invisible over the dot; one shared name per group.
.radio__dotRequired. The 22 px circle; checked fills accent with a white 8 px dot. aria-hidden.
.check__textRequired. The option text; a small inside adds a second line.

States

StateHow
checkedAccent fill with the primary finish; white center dot.
hoverEdge darkens to --text-subtle.
:activeThe dot sinks to 0.88 in 90 ms.
focus-visibleFocus ring around the dot.
disabledNative disabled; style the text yourself only if needed. Say why in a second line.

Motion and microinteractions

  • Selecting: the center dot scales 0 to 1 on --spring-snappy (360 ms); the fill and edge change in --d-fast (160 ms). The previous option shrinks its dot in --d-exit (180 ms) on --ease-exit, so the old choice is gone before the new one settles.
  • Press: scale(0.88) in 90 ms, back on --spring-snappy.
  • Reduced motion: transitions become 120 ms linear.

Accessibility

  • Real <input type="radio"> with a shared name: Tab enters the group at the checked option, arrow keys move and select, Space selects.
  • Wrap the group in <fieldset> with a <legend>; the question is read with each option.
  • Preselect a sensible default when one exists, so the group never sits empty without reason; otherwise validate with an error under the group.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Order options logically (frequency, size, time).
  • Keep labels short and parallel; put detail in the small line.

cancelDon't

  • Use radios for more than about five options.
  • Make a single radio; one choice that can be undone is a checkbox.
  • Lay several radios out in a wrapping row on phones.
