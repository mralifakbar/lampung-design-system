Event calendar

A calendar of events with three views: a month grid with event chips, a week of timed blocks with a now line, and an agenda list grouped by day. It renders from JSON and turns into dots plus the chosen day's list when it has less than 640 px.

Usage

Use it when

  • People plan around dates and times: a team calendar, class schedules, bookings, deadlines across projects.
  • Seeing what else happens that day or week matters: finding a free slot, spotting a crowded week.

Use something else when

  • Picking one date or a span in a form: use Calendar. Or date field and date range
  • A short list of upcoming items: use List. Group rows by day with a labeled separator
  • The history of one record: use Timeline.

Where it goes. Full width of the content column, as the main object of a page (a filter or calendar list can sit beside it). It sizes by its own width, not the screen: under 640 px the month shows dots and the selected day's events, and only Month and Agenda are offered.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.evcal[data-lds-events]Required. The host. data-date (shown date), data-view (month, week, agenda), data-week-start (1 = Monday), data-hours ("7-20"), data-locale, and for fixed examples data-today and data-now ("10:20").
script[type="application/json"]The events: id, title, start ("2026-11-16" or "2026-11-16T14:00"), optional end, allDay, tone (1 to 4, the chart series colors) and where.
.evcal__headRendered: the title (live region), previous, Today, next in a button group, and a segmented control for the view.
.evcal__month > .evcal__cellRendered month grid: a day button .evcal__date, up to three .evcal__evt chips, a "2 more" .evcal__more, and .evcal__dots for narrow widths.
.evcal__weekRendered week: day headers, an all-day row, an hour gutter and .evcal__day columns with .evcal__blocks side by side when they overlap, plus .evcal__now.
.evcal__agendaRendered agenda: .sep-label day headings that stick while their day scrolls, then .evcal__rows.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
data-view="month"Default. Chips with start time; multi-day events repeat on each day with a squared start.
data-view="week"Planning a week by the hour. Hidden under 640 px.
data-view="agenda"Reading what comes next; the default on phones when week was asked for.

States

StateHow
todayThe date sits in an accent circle (aria-current="date"); in week view the day header is accent text and the column has a faint tint.
outside the monthCells on the quiet fill with muted dates.
selected day (narrow)Accent tint on the cell; its events list under the grid.
emptyAgenda: "No events this month" with what to do; a narrow day: "Nothing planned for this day."
loadingRender after the data arrives; until then show a skeleton block the height of the month inside .evcal__body.

Motion and microinteractions

  • Moving to the next or previous month or week slides the body in from that side on --spring-smooth (view-in--forward and --back, 24 px), so direction is felt; changing view fades it in over --d-standard.
  • Day buttons and event chips sink on press (0.9 and 0.97) and spring back on --spring-snappy.
  • Reduced motion: views swap with a 120 ms fade.

Accessibility

  • The month is a role="grid" of rows and cells; each day is a real button labeled with the full date and its event count ("Monday 16 November, 3 events"), with one tab stop and arrow keys between days (moving past the edge pages the month).
  • Event chips and blocks are buttons labeled with title, time and place.
  • The title is a live region, so "December 2026" is announced after paging; previous and next say "Previous month" or "Previous week".
  • Tones are never the only cue: each event says its title and time in words.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Open event details in a popover or drawer on lds:event, and offer "New event" on lds:day.
  • Keep titles short; the place goes in where.

cancelDon't

  • Use more than four tones; group by calendar instead and let people filter with filter().
  • Squeeze the week view onto a phone; the agenda reads better.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. data-lds-events renders the calendar (or call eventCalendar(el, { events })). It fires lds:event { event, el }, lds:day { date, events, el } and lds:range { start, end, view } after every render. The handle from eventCalendar(el) has set(events), go(date), view(name), filter(fn) and today().
