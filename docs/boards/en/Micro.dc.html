Microinteractions

The small moments people touch all day: a like, a rename, a delete. Each one answers within 100 ms, says the result where the action happened, and gives back as much feedback as the event deserves, no more.

Trigger

Visible, labeled with its outcome, with distinct rest, hover, pressed, busy and disabled states. Every gesture has a button or key that does the same.

Rules

Few and predictable. Repeated presses are ignored while busy, a confirm cannot be hit by the same click that armed it, limits refuse input instead of failing later.

Feedback

On the control itself, first. A toast only when the result needs an Undo or happened somewhere else. Motion, words and haptics, never color alone.

Loops and modes

Hints retire after a few visits. Modes are rare, always visible, and one key away from leaving.

Feedback scales with the event

A hover gets a tint, a delete gets a sentence and an Undo. Try each one; none of them opens anything you have to close.

Gestures with a way in

Swipe a row left to reveal its actions; swipe far or flick to run the last one. The same actions open with the left arrow key, and a hint explains it for the first three visits only.
