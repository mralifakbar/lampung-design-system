Funnel

The steps of a flow, each with how many reached it and what share of the step before moved on. Centered bars narrow with the count, so the biggest drop is visible at a glance; the words carry the exact numbers.

Usage

Use it when

  • A sequence where each step is a subset of the one before: visit, try, sign up, pay; apply, screen, interview, offer.
  • Finding the step that loses the most people, to decide what to fix first.

Use something else when

  • Categories that are not nested steps: use Ranked bars.
  • Change of one count over time: use Area chart.
  • Parts of one whole: use Percentage bar.
  • The status of one record moving through steps: use Steps.

Where it goes. In a chart card, half or full width. Three to six steps. Words sit above each bar, so it works at 320 px without change.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
ol.funnelRequired. An ordered list with an aria-label; the order is the meaning.
li.funnel__rowRequired. One step. --v is its count as a percent of the first step.
.funnel__metaRequired. .funnel__label (the step), .funnel__val (the count) and .funnel__conv (share of the step before).
.funnel__track > .funnel__barRequired. A 28 px fill track with the bar centered in it; decorative (aria-hidden).
.is-dropOptional on a row: the step that loses most (or under your threshold); its share turns to danger text.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
.is-dropFlags the step with the weakest share; say it in the footer too.
--vBar width: count divided by the first step's count.

States

StateHow
data changeSet new --v and words: bars move over 480 ms on --ease-inout.
emptyKeep the step names; counts at 0 and one line: "No visits in this period".
loadingA .skel line and a 28 px .skel strip per step.

Motion and microinteractions

  • First view (data-lds-grow): bars grow outward from the center on --spring-smooth, 35 ms apart top to bottom.
  • Later changes: width over --d-considered on --ease-inout. Hover fades other bars over --d-fast.
  • Reduced motion: drawn at once.

Accessibility

  • An ol with aria-label, so the steps are announced in order with their counts and shares as text.
  • The bars are aria-hidden; they repeat the numbers.
  • The weakest step is named in words in the footer, not only by color.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Name steps as what people did ("Created an account").
  • Show the share of the step before, not of the first step; that is where the decision is.

cancelDon't

  • Draw trapezoids or 3D cones; centered bars are exact.
  • Use a funnel for steps people can skip or repeat.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. Optional: data-lds-grow on the list plays the first-view entrance.
