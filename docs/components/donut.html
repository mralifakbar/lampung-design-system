Donut

A ring split into up to four segments that show parts of one whole, with the total in the middle and a labelled list beside it. The list carries the numbers; the ring shows proportion at a glance.

Penggunaan

Pakai saat

  • Two to four parts of one whole where the reader cares about the rough split: students per grade, time per activity.
  • A dashboard tile where the total matters as much as the split (the total sits in the hole).

Pakai yang lain saat

  • Parts of a whole in a narrow row or with many categories: pakai Percentage bar.
  • Comparing sizes precisely or more than four parts: pakai Bar chart. Bars are easier to compare than angles.
  • Progress of one value toward a goal: pakai Progress ring.
  • Change over time: pakai Line chart.

Letaknya. In a .chart-card, ring on the left (168 px) and the list on the right; on phones the list wraps under the ring. Half width on dashboards.

Contoh

Anatomi

BagianFungsinya
.donut-chartThe component: ring and legend side by side, wrapping under 400 px. Drawn by data-lds-chart with type: "donut", or written by hand with the parts below.
.donut > svg > circle.donut__segOne circle per part (r 64 in a 168 viewBox, rotated so the first part starts at the top); stroke-dasharray sets its length, stroke-dashoffset its start, --series-n its color.
.donut__labelThe total (b) and what it counts (span), centered in the hole.
ul.donut-legendOne li per part: swatch i, name, value b, share em. Same order as the ring.
.donut-chart--thinA 10 px ring for small cards.
svg.donutRequired. 168 by 168 viewBox, rotated -90 degrees so the first segment starts at the top. role="img" + aria-label.
circleRequired. One per segment, r 64, fill: none, 14 px stroke (set by .donut circle). Length via stroke-dasharray, position via stroke-dashoffset, color via --series-n.
center labelThe total and its unit, absolutely centered over the svg (inline styles; no class yet).
listRequired. Swatch, label and value per segment, in the same order as the ring.

Status

StatusCaranya
data changeSet new stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset; both transition. Update the list, the total and the aria-label.
one segment is 100 %Show the ring whole in that color and say so in the list.
loading, empty, errorSee chart states; a loading donut is a .skel circle (border-radius: 50%).

Gerak dan mikrointeraksi

  • Segment length and position transition over --d-considered (480 ms) on --ease-inout; opacity over --d-fast for hover dimming you add.
  • First view (drawn from data, or data-lds-grow): every segment sweeps from zero to its length over 480 ms on --ease-inout.
  • Pointing at a segment or a legend row fades the other segments to 35 % and the other rows to 55 % over --d-fast.
  • Reduced motion: no sweep; changes take 120 ms.

Aksesibilitas

  • role="img" on the svg with every part and the total in the aria-label.
  • The list beside the ring is real text with the numbers; it is the accessible version and the precise one.
  • Segments are separated by a 2 unit gap and by order, not only by color.

Lakukan dan hindari

check_circleLakukan

  • Order segments by meaning (A to D) or by size, starting at the top.
  • Put the total in the hole and the numbers in the list.

cancelHindari

  • Use more than four segments; fold the smallest into Other.
  • Label segments on the ring itself.
  • Use a full pie or 3D.

Perilaku dan kode

HTML biasa. data-lds-chart='{ "type": "donut", "segments": [{ "name": "Search", "value": 540 }], "center": "1,284", "unit": "sign-ups", "label": "…" }' or drawChart(el, config); calling it again with the same number of parts moves the segments. data-lds-chart-for on a range control switches sets. Hand-written .donut-chart markup gets the linked hover from init().
