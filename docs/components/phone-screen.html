Phone screen

The standard phone layout: a large title that collapses into a translucent nav bar as content scrolls under it, grouped lists in the middle, and a translucent tab bar at the bottom. The bars are the only blurred surfaces.

Usage

Use it when

  • Designing or prototyping a phone app screen in the system: a home "Today" screen, a settings screen, a course list.
  • Showing a phone layout inside a desktop page (docs, previews, onboarding) with .phone.phone--inline.
  • A top-level screen that is one of three to five main areas reached from the tab bar.

Use something else when

  • A responsive web page that should work on phones and desktops: use App shell. The shell turns its sidebar into a drawer on phones.
  • Only the bars themselves (nav bar, tab bar) and their rules: use Phone bars.
  • A task that keeps the current screen in view (check in, filters): use Sheet.
  • A site with a few top-level pages and no app chrome: use Top nav.
  • A list of settings or links inside a screen: use List. The inset grouped list the screen is built from.

Where it goes. This is a whole screen. .phone is a 390 by 844 frame (the size of a common phone); .phone--inline is a smaller 320 by 520 frame with an edge and shadow for showing a screen inside a page. In a real phone web app, use the same parts in a full-height container that you position and size in the app stylesheet.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.phoneRequired. The frame: positioned, clipped, canvas background, 48 px radius. .phone--inline for an embedded preview.
.navbarRequired. Absolute bar at the top: a three-column grid (back or empty, .navbar__title, .navbar__end with icon buttons). Needs an id. Its material and title appear only after scrolling (.is-scrolled).
.phone__scroll[data-lds-navbar]Required. The scrolling content under both bars, data-lds-navbar set to the nav bar id.
spacer + h1.large-titleRequired. Space for the bar (100 px in .phone, or .status-gap plus the bar height), then the 34 px bold title, 20 px from the edge.
contentGrouped lists (.list.list--fill with .lrow), KPI cards, search; 16 to 20 px side padding, about 110 px bottom padding so the last row clears the tab bar.
nav.tabbar > button.tabbar__itemRequired on top-level screens. Three to five items with icon and label; aria-current="page" on the current one, which uses the filled icon.
.home-indOptional. The home indicator line, for mockups only.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
.phone390 by 844 frame for full-size mockups; the bars use their full heights (nav bar 100 px with 54 px for the status bar, tab bar 83 px with room for the home indicator).
.phone.phone--inline320 by 520 framed preview inside a page; shorten the bars with inline sizes as in the examples (64 px nav bar, 76 px tab bar), since there is no status bar.

States

StateHow
.navbar.is-scrolledSet by data-lds-navbar when content scrolls more than 44 px: material behind the bar and the small title appear.
.tabbar__item[aria-current="page"]Accent color and the filled icon, which pops once when it becomes current.

Motion and microinteractions

  • Nav bar material: opacity in --d-fast (160 ms) linear, tied to the 44 px scroll threshold, so it never half-shows.
  • Small title: opacity in --d-fast and a 6 px rise in --d-standard (240 ms) on --ease-enter. Scrolling back reverses it.
  • Tab bar: the pressed icon sinks to 0.86 on --spring-snappy; the new current icon pops with lds-pop (0.82 to 1) on --spring-snappy (360 ms). Switching tabs swaps the content without a slide (view swap).
  • Reduced transparency: both bars use the solid material without blur. Reduced motion: changes become 120 ms linear.

Accessibility

  • One h1 per screen: the large title. The small title in the bar repeats it visually; it can stay as plain text.
  • The tab bar is a <nav> with an aria-label; items are buttons (or links) with aria-current="page" on the current one, and always a text label under the icon.
  • Bar buttons are 44 px icon buttons with aria-labels.
  • Keep the bottom padding so the last row can scroll out from under the tab bar; nothing interactive should stay hidden behind it.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Use three to five tab bar items, each a top-level area.
  • Group rows into inset lists with 24 to 28 px between groups; put destructive rows in their own group at the end.
  • Keep blur to the two bars.

cancelDon't

  • Add a third blurred surface or a shadow under the bars.
  • Hide the tab bar labels, or use it for actions (that is a floating action or a toolbar).
  • Put a large title on screens pushed from a list; they use the small title in the bar with a back button.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. data-lds-navbar="navbar-id" on .phone__scroll: toggles .is-scrolled on the nav bar once the content scrolls past 44 px. Tab switching is yours.
