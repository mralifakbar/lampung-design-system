Trend delta

The change next to a figure: an arrow for the direction, a color for what it means, the amount in tabular figures and a sentence for screen readers. Direction and meaning are separate, because a falling number can be good news.

Usage

Use it when

  • A key figure on a dashboard compared with the previous period: revenue up 12.5 %, refunds down 3 %, sign-ups flat.
  • A row in a table or list where the change matters as much as the value: a product's sales this week, a course's pass rate.
  • A stat row or sparkline stat that needs one honest word about the trend.

Use something else when

  • The state of a record (Paid, Overdue): use Badge.
  • The trend over many points matters: use Sparkline stat. Add the delta under the number, not instead of the line.
  • A count of things that need the person: use Count.

Where it goes. Right after or under the figure it qualifies, on the same baseline, never on its own. In a stat row it goes in the sub line; in a table it gets its own right-aligned column.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.deltaRequired. Inline row: arrow icon, signed amount, and the .sr-only sentence.
.delta--up | --down | --flatRequired. Direction: the arrow_upward icon points up, turns 180 degrees down, or lies flat at 90 degrees.
.delta--good | --bad | --neutralRequired. Meaning: success, danger or subtle text color. Pick it from what the number means, not from the arrow.
.sr-onlyRequired. The words the arrow and color stand for: "up from last month".
.delta__noteOptional visible comparison after the amount ("vs October"), muted.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
.deltaInline text, for sub lines and table cells.
.delta--pillOn its tint, 22 px tall, beside a large figure.

States

StateHow
good--success-text (and --success-tint on a pill).
bad--danger-text (and --danger-tint).
neutral--text-subtle; for no change, or a change that is neither good nor bad.
.is-changedSet by setDelta(): the text rises in once.

Motion and microinteractions

  • When the direction flips, the arrow turns 180 degrees on --spring-smooth over --d-smooth; color follows at once.
  • setDelta() lets the new amount rise in over --d-standard on --ease-enter (the system text swap).
  • Reduced motion: no rise; the arrow still turns, which is a state, not decoration.

Accessibility

  • The arrow is aria-hidden; the .sr-only sentence says direction and comparison ("up from last month"), so color and arrow are never the only signal.
  • Use the true minus sign (−) for negative amounts and a plus sign for positive ones.
  • Success and danger text tokens hold 4.5:1 on the canvas, the fill and their tints in both themes.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Color by meaning: fewer refunds, faster response times and lower costs are good when they go down.
  • Say once on the page what the comparison is (last month, last week); repeat it only in the .sr-only text.

cancelDon't

  • Show a delta without the figure it qualifies.
  • Use the accent color for a trend, or red and green with no arrow and no words.
  • Round so much that a change shows as 0.0 % with a colored arrow.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. No hook needed for static markup. setDelta(el, change, { goodWhen, unit, note, digits }) rewrites a .delta: direction class, meaning class (from goodWhen, default data-good or "up"), signed amount with unit (default data-unit or "%") and the .sr-only sentence (from note, default data-note).
