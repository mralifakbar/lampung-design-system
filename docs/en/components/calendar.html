Calendar

A month grid of round day buttons with today in accent text and the selection in the accent fill; a range fills between its ends. The markup and states; picking logic is yours.

Usage

Use it when

  • Choosing a day or a range where seeing the week matters: booking a consultation slot, picking exam days, setting leave dates.
  • Showing which days in a month are marked (deadlines, sessions) in a side panel.

Use something else when

  • A date field in a form (birth date, a known date): use Date field. The date field opens this calendar in a popover and accepts typing.
  • A list of upcoming events: use List.
  • Events in order with progress: use Timeline.
  • Activity per day over months: use Heatmap.

Where it goes. In a side column or a popover, 300 px wide. Inside a date field popover, a sheet on phones, or beside a list of slots for the selected day on wide screens.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.calRequired. 300 px white box with a ring and 18 px radius. role="group" with an aria-label.
.cal__headMonth navigation: previous and next icon buttons and the .cal__month title.
.cal__monthRequired. "November 2026".
.cal__gridRequired. Seven columns.
.cal__dowWeekday initials row (aria-hidden; each day button carries its full label).
button.cal__dayRequired. A 38 px round day button with aria-label like "Friday 20 November 2026".
.is-today / .is-selected / .is-range / .is-mutedToday in accent text; the selected day (or range ends) filled; days between the ends on the tint; days outside the month faint.

States

StateHow
hoverDay tints --bg-tint-strong.
:activeDay sinks to scale(0.88) and springs back.
today.is-today plus aria-current="date".
selected.is-selected plus aria-pressed="true".
in range.is-range: square tint across the cell width.
outside the month / unavailable.is-muted; for days that cannot be picked also set disabled and say why near the calendar.

Motion and microinteractions

  • Day color changes over --d-fast (160 ms) on --ease-standard; press sinks to 0.88 and returns on --spring-snappy.
  • Changing months swaps the grid at once; if you animate it, use a short view swap, never a long slide.

Accessibility

  • Each day is a <button> with a full aria-label (weekday, day, month, year); weekday initials are aria-hidden.
  • Mark today with aria-current="date" and the selection with aria-pressed="true" (or aria-selected in a role="grid" you build fully).
  • Keyboard: buttons are reachable with Tab. A full date-picker grid (arrow keys move by day, Page Up and Page Down by month, one tab stop) is app logic you add; lds.js does not ship it.
  • Announce the month when it changes (aria-live="polite" on .cal__month).

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Start the week on the day your audience expects.
  • Show the chosen date or range in words next to the calendar ("18 to 22 November").
  • Disable days that cannot be chosen and say why.

cancelDon't

  • Use a calendar for a birth date; use a date field that accepts typing.
  • Color days by meaning without a legend in words.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. None. Selecting days, ranges and moving between months are app logic: update the classes and ARIA states listed above.
