Gauge New

A half ring with the value in its opening: one figure against its known range (seats booked, a score, a load), or a row of ticks shared out between a few states (in stock, low, sold out). Reads at a glance; the words under the number carry the detail.

Usage

Use it when

  • One value against a fixed range where "how full" is the question: capacity booked, quota used, a health score, a server's load.
  • A small set of states that share one total and the main share matters most: stock in, low, out; tickets open, waiting, closed.
  • A dashboard tile with room for a shape but not for a chart.

Use something else when

  • A score in a small square (a card corner, a list row): use Progress ring.
  • Storage or seats in a narrow row: use Meter.
  • Progress toward a goal with a deadline: use Target bar.
  • Parts of a whole where every part matters: use Donut.
  • Change over time: use Area chart.

Where it goes. In a chart card or a KPI card-sized tile, centered, 160 to 240 px wide (--size). It scales down with its container; the number stays 28 px.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.gaugeRequired. --size sets the width; the svg keeps a 200 × 112 viewBox.
svg[role=img]Required. aria-label states the value and the range in words.
.gauge__track / .gauge__valOne value: a 16 px track and the value arc (--v as a percent), in the accent or a tone.
.gauge__tickShared ticks: 32 short marks, each colored by the part it belongs to (--c).
.gauge__labelRequired. b for the number, span for what it counts.
.gauge__endsOptional. The two ends of the range under the ring.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
value / maxOne arc. tone: "success" | "warning" | "danger" or .gauge--success|warning|danger on static markup.
segmentsTicks shared by up to four parts in series order; ticks changes the count (default 32).
center / unitThe number in the opening and what it counts; defaults to the formatted value.
--sizeWidth of the gauge (default 220 px).

States

StateHow
data changedrawChart(el, { type: "gauge", value }) or a range control: the arc moves from where it is.
loadingA .skel half ring is enough: a 220 × 110 block with a 110 px top radius.
over the maxCap the arc at the end, say the overshoot in the unit line ("12 over capacity").

Motion and microinteractions

  • First view: the arc sweeps from zero over --d-considered (480 ms) on --ease-inout; ticks take their color one after another, 12 ms apart.
  • Value changes and tone changes run on the same transition, from the current position.
  • Reduced motion: drawn at once.

Accessibility

  • The svg is role="img" with value and range in words; the number and unit are real text too.
  • Tones repeat the meaning in words ("nearly full"); they are never the only signal.
  • Shared ticks always come with a legend that has the counts.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Use it for one figure with a meaningful maximum.
  • Write the unit line as words people would say ("78 of 120 seats").

cancelDon't

  • Add a needle, colored zones or tick numbers around the ring.
  • Put several gauges in one card to compare them; use ranked bars.
  • Use a gauge for values without a fixed maximum.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. data-lds-chart='{ "type": "gauge", "value": 78, "max": 120, "label": "…" }' or drawChart(el, config). data-lds-chart-for on a range control switches sets.
