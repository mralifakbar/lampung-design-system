Separator

A 1 px hairline between two blocks of content that would otherwise run together, plain or with a few words in it (a date divider, "or"). Most of the time spacing or the component's own dividers are enough.

Penggunaan

Pakai saat

  • Two different groups inside one surface (a card, a panel, a menu-like area) that are not rows of a list.
  • Between a block of content and its footer inside a card.
  • Labeled: dividing a feed, a chat or an agenda by day ("Today", "Monday, 16 November"), or "or" between two ways to sign in.

Pakai yang lain saat

  • Between rows of a list: pakai List. Lists draw their own inset hairlines.
  • Between key-value rows: pakai Key-value.
  • Between page sections: pakai Sections. Sections draw a hairline above each one after the first.
  • Between items in a menu: pakai Dropdown menu. Use .menu__sep.

Letaknya. Inside a surface, edge to edge of its content, with at least 12 px of space above and below. Never on the page canvas between sections.

Contoh

Anatomi

BagianFungsinya
hr.sepRequired. A 1 px block in --hairline, no margin of its own.
.sep--vA vertical hairline between items in a row (a toolbar, an app bar), at least 20 px tall.
.sep-labelA labeled separator: a heading (h3) or a <p> whose text sits between two hairlines in the caption style. Optional .count or .badge inside.

Varian dan ukuran

Kelas atau atributKapan
.sepHorizontal hairline.
.sep--vVertical hairline in a row.
.sep-labelWords centered between two hairlines.
.sep-label--startWords at the start, one hairline after them: date dividers that line up with rows.
.sep-label--stickyStays at the top of its scrolling container (--sticky-top offsets it under a sticky bar) on the canvas color.

Gerak dan mikrointeraksi

  • None.

Aksesibilitas

  • Use <hr> when the break is a change of topic (it is announced as a separator); add aria-hidden="true" when it is only visual, so it is not announced.
  • A labeled separator that names a group (a day) is a heading, so people can jump between days; "or" between two actions is a plain <p>.

Lakukan dan hindari

check_circleLakukan

  • Try spacing first; add a hairline only when spacing is not enough.
  • Space it with the layout (stack gaps or margins), never with padding on the separator.

cancelHindari

  • Use thicker or colored lines.
  • Stack a separator next to a list hairline or a section hairline.

Terkait

Tampilan dataListThe inset grouped list: rows on one surface, separated by hairlines that start where the text starts. The default for any list of things, instead of a stack of cards.Pola halamanSectionsA page as a column of titled sections: 48 px apart, a hairline above every section after the first, a larger heading on wide screens. Structure comes from space and headings, not from a card around each part.Tampilan dataCardA surface that holds one subject: an event, a payment, a course. White with a hairline ring on the canvas, or the quiet fill on white. Never a shadow.LandasanLayout primitivesSeven small classes that lay out everything between components: `stack`, `cluster`, `auto-grid`, `split-2`, `center`, `measure` and `sr-only`. Reach for them before writing `display: flex` or `grid` by hand.
