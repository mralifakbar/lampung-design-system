Lampung Design System

A white canvas, one Lampung green, siger gold for identity, and type that tightens as it grows. Grouping comes from a quiet fill, depth only from layers that float, and every moving part runs on a spring you can interrupt.

01

White first

Cards and lists sit on a #F3F5F4 fill. Shadows are kept for sheets, menus, dialogs and toasts, the layers that actually float.

02

One green, used sparingly

#0B6E4F marks the primary action, the current selection and links. If a screen has three green things, two of them are wrong.

03

Type carries hierarchy

Size, weight and tracking move together. Large text tightens, small text opens up, body stays at zero.

04

Calm, interruptible motion

Things arrive on a critically damped spring and leave faster than they came. Nothing loops unless it reports a live state.

Accent

The fill is #0B6E4F in light and #0F7D59 in dark; white on either holds at least 5:1. Text set in the accent switches to a lighter shade on dark.

  • FillPrimary button, switch on, selected day.
  • TextLinks and tinted buttons, 6.7:1 on white.
