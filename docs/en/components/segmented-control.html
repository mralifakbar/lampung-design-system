Segmented control

Two to five equal segments with one thumb that slides to the chosen one. For switching the view or mode of what is on screen right now; the change applies at once.

descriptionView as Markdown

Usage

Use it when

  • Switching the period or view of the same content: Day, Week, Month; List, Grid; Chart, Table.
  • A mode with two to four named states that applies immediately: Light, Dark, System.
  • A compact single choice in a toolbar or a card header.

Use something else when

  • Each choice shows a different panel of content (Overview, Files, History): use Tabs. Tabs carry tab semantics and panels.
  • More than five options, or long labels: use Select.
  • A single on and off setting: use Switch.
  • A choice saved with a form, needing explanations: use Radio. Or radio cards
  • Several options can be on at once (filters): use Chips.
  • A row of toolbar actions: use Button group.

Where it goes. Above or in the header of the content it changes: over a chart or a list, in a card header, in a toolbar. Full width on phones under the large title; on wide screens size it to its labels (about 90 px per segment) instead of stretching across the page.

Examples

Anatomy

PartWhat it is
.segRequired. The recessed track; role="group" with an aria-label; data-lds-segmented for the behavior.
.seg__thumbRequired. The raised thumb under the chosen segment. The JS sets its width to one segment and moves it.
button.seg__btnRequired. One segment, equal width, aria-pressed. Hairline separators between unpressed neighbors.

Variants and sizes

Class or attributeWhen
seg (default)30 px segments, 13 px labels. Toolbars and card headers.
seg--lg36 px segments, 14 px labels. The main control of a screen; the React Segmented default.

States

StateHow
aria-pressed="true"The chosen segment: semibold label over the thumb; separators next to it hide.
:activeThe label dims to 60 % while pressed.
focus-visibleFocus ring on the focused segment.

Motion and microinteractions

  • The thumb slides with transform: translateX() on --spring-smooth (560 ms). A new choice during travel retargets from the current position; it never jumps back. See sliding indicator.
  • Separators next to the chosen segment fade in --d-fast (160 ms).
  • The thumb keeps its spring even while the theme swaps, so the appearance control never snaps.
  • Reduced motion: the thumb moves in 120 ms linear.

Accessibility

  • A role="group" with an aria-label; segments are <button type="button"> with aria-pressed, so each is announced as a pressed or not pressed toggle.
  • Arrow keys (Left, Right, Up, Down) move the choice and focus with wrap-around; Tab enters each segment.
  • When the segments switch panels of content, use tabs instead so role="tab" and aria-controls connect them.
  • Icons are decorative; the text is the name. An icon-only segment needs an aria-label.

Do and don't

check_circleDo

  • Keep labels to one or two short words of similar length.
  • Apply the change immediately and keep the choice when people come back.

cancelDon't

  • Use it to navigate between pages.
  • Put more than five segments, or let labels truncate.
  • Build a second sliding-thumb control by hand; reuse .seg.

Behavior and code

Plain HTML. data-lds-segmented on .seg: sizes the thumb to one segment, moves it to the aria-pressed="true" segment, handles click and arrow keys, and fires lds:change with { index, item } on the group.

React. From lampung-design-system/react.

import { Segmented } from 'lampung-design-system/react';

<Segmented
  label="Period"
  value={period}
  onChange={setPeriod}
  size="md"
  options={[{ value: 'day', label: 'Day' }, { value: 'week', label: 'Week' }, { value: 'month', label: 'Month' }]}
/>
